rclone-pc-backup

A personal rclone setup for backing up one PC to its owner's Google Drive.

This site exists to document the OAuth application used by rclone-pc-backup. It is not a public backup service, has no sign-up, and is not offered to third parties.

What it does

The app connects rclone to the owner's Google Drive with the drive.file scope. It creates and manages only the files it uploads, under a dedicated backup folder.

Data access

The app can view, create, modify, and delete Google Drive files that it created or that were opened through the app. It cannot browse the rest of the Drive library.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. In short: no data collection, no analytics, no sharing, and no third-party servers.
